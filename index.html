Our Automation Onboarding Process

At Tubexh, we follow a proven 4-step process to get your AI automation live fast. We handle everything — from strategy to setup to go-live — keeping you informed and in control at every stage.

1

Step 1 – Discovery & Planning

We start with a deep-dive call to understand your business, sales process, and pain points. We map out which tasks can be automated — lead follow-ups, appointment booking, customer queries — and define clear, measurable goals for your automation system.

2

Step 2 – Website Design & Mockups

Our team builds your AI agents, CRM workflows, and automation pipelines. We configure WhatsApp bots, lead capture forms, follow-up sequences, and platform integrations — all fully customised for your business and brand voice.

3

Step 3 – Website Design Development & Building

Once you’ve approved the design, we begin the development phase. We transform approved mockups into a fully functional, responsive site using clean, modern code. This includes responsive coding for all devices, CMS integration for easy content updates, and security implementation. Throughout development, we conduct rigorous testing across different browsers to ensure flawless functionality.

4

Step 4 – Website Launch & Optimisation

The final step is launch and ongoing optimisation. Before going live, we conduct final testing, set up analytics tracking, and submit your sitemap to search engines. After launch, we don't just disappear – we monitor performance, provide training on updating content, and remain available for support. Our commitment extends well beyond launch day, ensuring your investment continues to deliver results for years to come.
